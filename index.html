prime drifter organ — field guide

the idea

one root note that drifts by pure prime ratios. every interval you play is an exact fraction — 3:2, 5:4, 7:4 — tuned fresh against wherever your root happens to be right now. nothing is ever slightly out of tune; "in tune" just follows you around.

before keyboards-with-fixed-tuning, every key meant a fixed pitch. this instrument retunes between every note instead, like a slide or a voice — equal temperament never enters into it.

the vector

your position is four exponents — powers of 2, 3, 5, 7. up a fifth is [-1, 1, 0, 0]; then up a fourth [2, -1, 0, 0] and you land exactly on an octave [1, 0, 0, 0]. frequencies are computed from the vector, never accumulated from floats, so there is zero drift. octave folding only keeps things audible — it never changes the note.

right hand — voicing

l sus4 = 4:3 + 3:2 · ; wide = +2 · ' tenth = 3:2 + 5:2

one chord at a time, , clears it. yuiop stack extra ratios (9:8, 5:4, 4:3, 3:2, 7:4) — and a stack that matches a chord voice cancels it instead (XOR), so you can peel a triad apart.

sound

hold space — the organ breathes through whatever is armed. chord and root changes morph voices in and out mid-note. sliders shape attack, decay, sustain, release.

the initial root slider picks where zero lives (55–880 Hz) — the hum, the 0 key, and undo all follow it home.

the brightness slider opens the pipe's harmonic stack. Low is airy breath; higher is a small reed where pure intervals ring glassy still and mistunings buzz — the partials are what the tuning acts through. Watch the overtones panel while you play it: gold dots are partials landing dead-on, red ✕ are the near-misses that buzz.

the playback panel records your keystrokes as an editable text score — and plays any score back through the very same keys. The organ writes its own sheet music; edit the score to compose.

modes

. hum — quiet drone of your original root, so you can hear how far you've drifted.

n pivot — when a chord finishes, the root steps to that chord's outermost voice. already holding a note? , collapses the chord onto that voice and it keeps ringing as the new root.

/ hold — chord keys only sound while physically pressed. lines, not latches.

m invert — chords hang below the root (major becomes 4:5 + 2:3) and the pivot walks down.

safe exploring

0 back to the starting root · ⌫ undo the last move · \ redo. pivots are undoable too, so a chord chain retraces cleanly.

tips

the key grid below is clickable and hoverable. modes stack — hold + invert + pivot is a whole different instrument. the 0 key is always a safety net. play a chain of chords in pivot mode and watch the vector walk.
